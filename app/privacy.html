Privacy Policy

CalorieMate (the "app") · Effective 18 September 2026 · Designed by Odos

Summary

CalorieMate does not collect, store or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

1. Data you enter

Your profile (age, sex, height, weight, activity level, ethnicity setting, target weight), food and water logs, weight history, custom foods and preferences are stored only in your browser's or the app's local storage on your device. We have no server that receives this data and no way to access it. Deleting the app, clearing site data, or using Me → Reset app removes it. You can export a backup file yourself from Me → Your data; that file is created on your device and is not sent anywhere.

2. Voice input

When you use the microphone button, speech is converted to text by your device's speech-recognition service (the browser's Web Speech API, or the Android system speech service). The app receives only the resulting text. The app does not record, store or transmit audio. Your device or browser vendor's own privacy policy applies to that speech service.

3. Network requests the app makes

  • Loading the app and food database updates from GitHub Pages (rash914.github.io). These requests contain no personal data; standard server logs (IP address, time) may be kept by GitHub under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • "Search online (Open Food Facts)" — only when you tap it. The words you searched for are sent to Open Food Facts, a non-profit open database, to return product nutrition. No other data is sent.
  • Web font (Google Fonts) on the website version, subject to Google's privacy policy. The app works without it.

The app makes no other network requests.

4. Permissions (Android)

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — only for voice logging, only while you hold/tap the mic.
  • Internet — for the items in section 3.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

6. Health disclaimer

Nutrition values are estimates; targets and plans are general guidance, not medical advice. Consult a doctor before changing your diet if you are pregnant, under 18, or managing a medical condition.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.
